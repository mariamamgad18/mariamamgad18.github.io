About me

I'm a junior Flutter developer with a computer & data science background from Alexandria University, and hands-on experience building apps end to end — from state management and API integration to the small UI details that make an app feel finished.

I like structure: Clean Architecture, BLoC/Cubit, and dependency injection show up in most of what I build, because they keep projects easy to test and easier to hand off. Outside of code, I'm always looking for the next thing to learn.

Clean architecture isn't extra work — it's the work that saves you later.
